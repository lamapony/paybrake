What is real here

Rules check
Runs on your device inside the extension. Each card on this page is what the rules return for that chat, word for word.
AI check
In the extension, a second opinion from the demo server’s AI on masked text. It is rate-limited; without it the rules still work. This preview does not call it.
PayPal
Orders v2: PayPal sandbox when the server has sandbox keys, otherwise a mock. No real money moves. This preview makes no orders; the extension does.
