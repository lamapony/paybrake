PayBrake warns you before you pay a possible scammer

When a seller in a marketplace chat pushes you to pay in a risky way, for example a bank transfer or "Friends and Family" before you have seen the item, PayBrake shows a warning right in the chat.

It explains what looks wrong and suggests paying with a PayPal order instead. That order may be eligible for Buyer Protection; see PayPal terms.

Try the demo store

The demo store is a pretend marketplace. Fictional people, test orders, no real money.

What to try (about a minute)

  1. In the demo store, the bike chat is open. A PayBrake card pops up in the chat and says High risk.
  2. Click Switch to a PayPal order. PayBrake makes a test order.
  3. Click Open approval, then Approve (mock). Go back to the chat tab and click I approved it, capture.
  4. Bonus: open the camera chat and click Pay anyway. PayBrake lets you, and notes it on this device.

Good to know

  • AI check on all chats is on in this demo build. PayBrake's rules run inside your browser. It also asks an AI model about each chat, so it can catch scams the rules miss (fake bank or support messages, phishing links, requests for card details or MitID codes). Before a chat leaves your browser, emails, phone numbers, card numbers, IBANs (all but the last 4), ID numbers and codes are masked. The free test server passes the masked chat to its AI provider (Typesafe) and keeps nothing. You can turn it off in Settings; then no chat text leaves your browser.
  • When you make an order, only the order details (item, price, seller email) go to the test server.
  • This test version only works on the demo store.
