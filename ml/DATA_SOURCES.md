override with `$PAYBRAKE_DATASETS`). Hand-labeled `data/labeled/seed.jsonl` is the evaluation set.

### TOMATO label mapping (labels are per user/session, not per message)
- **scam** = every row of `answers/ATO_admessages.csv` (messages sent from a fraudster session, scenarios 1 phishing/off-platform, 2 overpayment/delivery fee, 3 spam).
- **honest** = `ad_messages.csv` rows from users **not** in `answers/ato_users.csv` **and** whose text never appears among scam texts (1,071 template texts are shared with the fraud set; those 32,394 rows are dropped as ambiguous).
- **dropped** = legitimate-session rows of the 70 ATO users (49,510 rows).
- conversation = all messages from one sender about one ad (`user_id, adid`), newline-joined (TOMATO has no replies); deduplicated by text; 3000 sampled per class (seed 0). See `load_tomato()` in `ml/pretrain_data.py`.

### Danish synthetic dialogs
`data/labeled/da_synthetic.jsonl` (152 rows: 72 scam / 80 honest, `source=synthetic`) is generated by `ml/gen_da_synthetic.py` from patterns paraphrased (not copied) from public warnings: politi.dk (Københavns Politi 2022-08-30; "Undgå at blive svindlet, når du handler med andre på nettet"), support.dba.dk fake-buyer articles, sikkerdigital.dk "Vær opmærksom på svindlere", tjekdet.dk guide. Kept separate from the hand seed (`common.default_paths()` excludes it).
