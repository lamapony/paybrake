The scam is one sentence long. PayBrake reads it before you pay.

PayBrake is a Chrome extension for marketplace chats. When a seller asks for a deposit, Friends & Family or a bank transfer, a card appears under the message, quotes the words that set it off, and offers a PayPal Goods & Services order for the same item.

The blue button builds one order for the item and the price in the chat. You approve it on PayPal’s page, come back to the chat and capture it. The card then reads: Paid with PayPal Goods & Services (sandbox). Buyer Protection may be eligible; see PayPal terms.

This build runs on the PayPal sandbox or on a mock. Live PayPal is refused in code, and no real money moves.

Five more messages.
Three get a card,
two get nothing.

These lines come from the repo’s test cases and demo chats. The verdicts are what rules v5 returned on 9 Nov 2026. A wrong warning teaches people to ignore the next one, so PayBrake waits for a payment request it can quote.

  1. Please pay with F&F, it is 1,800 kr. Several people are asking so today only.

    PayBrake marked: F&F, Several people are asking, today only.

    High risk payment request

    Friends & Family for goods: if the item never arrives, you may have no way to get your money back.

    Friends & Family · Pressure to hurry

  2. I'm abroad so I can only ship. Bank transfer of 1,800 kr first and the tent is yours.

    PayBrake marked: I'm abroad, can only ship, Bank transfer of 1,800 kr first.

    High risk payment request

    Payment before you see the item: if it never arrives, the money may be hard to get back.

    Off-platform payment · Pay before seeing the item · Ships before you meet

  3. Buy a €100 gift card and send me the code. That is the only way I can take payment.

    PayBrake marked: gift card, send me the code.

    High risk payment request

    Never share the code or card details with anyone: your bank, PayPal or a real buyer never asks.

    Off-platform payment · Asks for card details, a code or a password

  4. Yes, but today only. I'm in Hamburg for work until the 14th, so I can only ship.

    PayBrake marked as weak signals: today only, can only ship.

    No card

    Hurry and shipping-only are weak signals. With no payment request in the message, the rules stay quiet.

  5. Sure, pay through the Marketplace checkout or cash when you pick it up. I'm home after 17:00 most days.

    PayBrake marked as safe ways to pay: through the Marketplace checkout, cash when you pick.

    No card

    Checkout or cash at pickup is a safe way to pay, so the rules stay quiet.

Checked in this tab with the same rules file the extension ships. Nothing is sent anywhere. Rules only: the extension also runs its on-device model and, when it is switched on, the server AI check.

The verdict appears here.

Filled: a payment signal. Amber underline: a weak signal that cannot raise a card on its own. Blue underline: a safe way to pay. In a real chat the extension gives every phrase it quotes the same highlight, as at the top of this page.

Two checks run on your device. A third can run on a server.

Rules On-device model Server AI check
Where it runs In the extension In the extension On the server, when the AI check is on
What it reads The other person’s lines, in English and Danish Chats that have not asked for money yet A copy with emails, numbers, IBANs and codes masked
What it can do Raise a card and quote the words that set it off Add a medium card at most Confirm, lower or clear a rules card, or add a card where the rules found nothing
What it cannot do React to your own messages Send anything anywhere Raise a rules card’s level

The on-device model is a small logistic classifier over hashed n-grams, shipped inside the extension. If the server is down, the two checks on your device carry on.

Try it on the bike chat.

About 2 minutes, nothing to run locally.

  1. Download the extension zip and unzip it.
  2. In Chrome, Edge or Brave open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the folder with manifest.json.
  3. Open the demo store. The card appears under Jonas’s message.
