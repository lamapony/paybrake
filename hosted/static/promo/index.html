Brakes before you pay.

PayBrake is a Chrome extension that flags risky payment requests in marketplace chats and offers a PayPal Goods & Services checkout instead.

Try the demo Install the extension GitHub

Hackathon build. Fictional names, PayPal sandbox, no real money. Not affiliated with PayPal or any marketplace.

  1. The message that should stop you. A 500 kr deposit, on Friends & Family, for a bike that ships before you have seen it.
  2. PayBrake says why, in plain words. The card sits under the message and names four signals: Friends & Family, an upfront deposit, pressure to hurry, shipping before you meet.
  3. You decide how to pay. Switch to a PayPal order, or pay anyway. Pay anyway steps aside and only notes it on your device.

The scam is one sentence long.

Everything before it is scenery: the good price, the three other buyers, the week in Aarhus. Then comes the request to pay the wrong way.

  1. Friends & Family

    Please pay with F&F, it is 1,800 kr. Several people are asking so today only.

    High risk. Friends & Family, pressure to hurry.

  2. Blind transfer

    I’m abroad so I can only ship. Bank transfer of 1,800 kr first and the tent is yours.

    High risk. Off-platform payment, pay before seeing the item, ships before you meet.

  3. Gift card

    Buy a €100 gift card and send me the code. That is the only way I can take payment.

    High risk. Off-platform payment, asks for card details, a code or a password.

These lines are test cases in the repo. PayBrake flagged all three as high risk on 9 Nov 2026.

Two checks run on your device. One can run on a server.

Rules

English and Danish patterns look for the payment ask itself, in the other person’s lines only. Every card quotes the words that set it off.

On-device model

A small classifier inside the extension, trained on synthetic chats. It sends nothing anywhere. It reads chats that have not asked for money yet and can add a medium card at most.

Server AI check

Gets a copy with emails, numbers, IBANs and codes masked. It can confirm, lower or clear a rules card, or add one where the rules found nothing. It can never raise a rules card’s level. If the server is down, the two checks above carry on.

Most chats get nothing.

A desk lamp, paid for at pickup. A wrong warning teaches people to ignore the next one, so PayBrake needs a quotable payment ask before it says anything.

18 of 18 scam chats flagged, on the repo’s own 39-chat test set.

0 of 21 honest chats flagged, on that same set.

362 of 713 scams caught by rules plus model, on 1,573 messages from four public datasets.

11 of 860 honest messages flagged, on those same datasets.

Measured 9 Nov 2026 with npm run eval and npm run eval:real:model. We wrote the 39-chat set ourselves, so treat it as a regression check. None of this is real marketplace traffic yet.

Same bike. Same price. A different way to pay.

Switch to a PayPal order builds a Goods & Services order for the item and the price in the chat, through PayPal Orders v2. You approve it, then capture it.

This build runs in the PayPal sandbox or on a mock, and live PayPal is refused in code. Buyer Protection may be eligible; PayPal’s terms decide.

Try it on the bike scam.

  1. Download the extension zip and unzip it.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the folder with manifest.json.
  3. Open the demo store. The card appears under the seller’s message.
