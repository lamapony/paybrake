Try PayBrake in 2 minutes

PayBrake is a Chrome extension that brakes before you pay a scammer. It reads a marketplace chat with on-device rules plus an AI check. When a seller pushes a risky payment (PayPal Friends & Family, a bank transfer, gift cards, codes), PayBrake quotes the line that worried it and offers a PayPal Goods & Services order (sandbox) instead.

  • PayPal Orders v2 (sandbox / mock)
  • AI scam check (Typesafe)
  • Chrome extension, MV3

Two steps: load the extension, then open the demo store. Fictional marketplace and names, not affiliated with PayPal. Test orders only: no real money moves, and nothing runs on your machine except the extension.

1. Get the extension (one zip)

Download paybrake-extension.zip

  1. Unzip it. You get a folder paybrake-extension with manifest.json inside.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Chrome, Edge or Brave) and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the paybrake-extension folder (the one with manifest.json).

A Welcome to PayBrake tab opens. If Chrome says "Manifest file is missing", go one folder deeper.

2. Try the demo store

Open the demo storeSafe chats (stay quiet)

  1. In the bike chat a PayBrake card says High risk and lists what it noticed. The AI checked tag shows the AI verdict came back.
  2. Click Switch to a PayPal order, then Open approval and Approve (mock). If this server runs on PayPal sandbox, Open approval goes to PayPal's sandbox approval page instead.
  3. Back in the chat click I approved it, capture: Paid with PayPal Goods & Services (sandbox).
  4. The desk lamp chat is a normal chat and stays quiet. Bonus: open the camera chat and press Pay anyway. PayBrake steps aside and logs the override on this device only.
About the AI check. Chats are masked in the browser (emails, phones, card numbers, IBANs, codes) before the AI check sees them. The key stays on this server. The demo AI check has a per-visitor rate limit and a daily cap; when it is reached, PayBrake keeps working with its on-device rules. Server status, including whether orders run on PayPal sandbox or the mock: /health.
